**Dentures sit on your gums and come out; dental implants are anchored in your jawbone and stay put.** That one difference drives almost everything else — how well you can chew, whether your teeth move when you talk, and what happens to your jawbone over time. Many denture wearers don't have to choose between the two: implants can also hold a denture firmly in place.

## Why do people look beyond dentures?

**Because dentures can move.** Some people have trouble chewing certain foods because their dentures shift in the mouth. Others worry about them lifting while speaking or laughing. And because a denture rests on the gums, the bone underneath keeps shrinking, so dentures tend to loosen over time and need relines.

## What makes dental implants different?

**An implant is a titanium post placed in the jawbone that acts like a tooth root.** Replacement teeth attach to the implants, so they don't rely on suction or adhesive. Because chewing forces reach the bone through the implants, they also help preserve the jawbone that dentures can't.

## Are dental implants comfortable?

**Once the mouth has healed, most patients say implants look and feel like natural teeth.** Because they are fixed in place, they don't rub or slip while eating or talking. Some soreness or swelling right after surgery is normal and usually settles within days.

## Are dentures the only choice for missing teeth?

**No.** There are several options, and the right one depends on your oral health, bone support, lifestyle, and budget. A dental professional checks all of these before recommending implants, which is why we start with a free consultation and a 3D scan.

## How do you care for dental implants?

**Brush, clean around the implants, and keep your regular check-ups.** Implants can last a long time with good home care. Your team will show you how to clean under a fixed bridge or around snap-on attachments.
