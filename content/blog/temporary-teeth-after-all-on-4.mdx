## What were the first teeth in the original series?

Maló, Rangert, and Nobre delivered an acrylic prosthesis, reinforced by a metal strip, within two hours after the four lower implants were placed. It was designed to hold a minimum of 10 teeth, because the tilted back implants moved the screw holes forward, usually to about the second premolar. Prosthesis survival in the series was 100%.<Cite id="malo-2003" />

Patzelt, Bahat, Reynolds, and Strub reviewed 13 papers and 4,804 implants, of which 74 failed, 74% of those failures inside the first 12 months. They counted 1,201 prostheses incorporated within 48 hours. Mean cumulative survival at 36 months was 99.0% for implants (standard deviation 1.0) and 99.9% for prostheses (standard deviation 0.3). Mean bone loss was 1.3 mm (standard deviation 0.4). They found no significant difference between upper and lower jaws, or between straight and tilted implants. They also wrote that the evidence was limited, that studies of five years or more were scarce, and that randomized trials were needed. The complication they single out is fracture of the all-acrylic bridge.<Cite id="patzelt-2014" />

The day-one visit itself, including what "same day" does and does not include, is in [same-day implants and new teeth in one day](/blog/same-day-dental-implants-new-teeth-one-day). The sore-mouth weeks that follow are in [the first weeks after All-on-4](/blog/all-on-4-recovery-first-weeks).

## Why did some of those acrylic teeth fracture?

Nine acrylic prostheses in the development group, 30%, had repairable fractures. Screw fracture or loosening sometimes came with those breaks. The routine-group bridges were then made with more material thickness, and the acrylic was cured under higher pressure. That group had no fractures in the reported follow-up. The authors wrote that a reinforced acrylic prosthesis might be used as a lower-cost final in that protocol. They also built metal or milled titanium finals for the development group, which is a different object from the two-hour acrylic.<Cite id="malo-2003" />

A fracture of the plastic teeth in that series did not, by itself, mean the bridge was lost. The implants were scored on their own life table. When one immediately loaded implant failed, the provisional survived on the other three.<Cite id="malo-2003" />

## Why does Renew call the day-one teeth temporary?

The [All-on-4 dental implants](/services/all-on-4-dental-implants) page says most patients receive implants and a full set of temporary teeth in a single visit, and that the permanent bridge is placed within a few months, after the implants integrate. People leave with teeth on day one. The whole treatment is still staged.<Cite id="renew-all-on-4" /> When that later bridge is made is covered in [when the final teeth are made after All-on-4](/blog/when-do-you-get-final-teeth-after-all-on-4).

The implant surgeon places the implants. Tom Szarski, DD, is the denturist who plans the teeth with the on-site lab. Calling the first set temporary is how this clinic separates the day-one acrylic-style bridge from the bridge made after integration. It is not a promise about how either set will feel in a particular mouth.
