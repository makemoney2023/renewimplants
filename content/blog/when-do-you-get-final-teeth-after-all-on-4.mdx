## When did the original series make a final prosthesis?

Maló, Rangert, and Nobre split the 44 lower jaws. For the development group, final prostheses were made 4 to 6 months after surgery. The first seven patients had conventional cast-metal frameworks. The other 23 had milled titanium Procera implant bridges. Those finals incorporated the rescue implants that had been left out of the provisional. The paper's figure caption also describes a final prosthesis made within 6 months using the Procera implant bridge.<Cite id="malo-2003" />

The routine group is the important limit. Those patients kept the provisional acrylic for the follow-up the authors reported, aside from two who received titanium frameworks after 12 months. The authors also wrote that a reinforced acrylic bridge might serve as a final in that protocol. Reporting only "4 to 6 months" would hide that second path.<Cite id="malo-2003" />

What the two-hour acrylic was, and why nine of the early ones fractured, is in [temporary teeth after All-on-4](/blog/temporary-teeth-after-all-on-4). The surgery-day visit is in [same-day implants and new teeth in one day](/blog/same-day-dental-implants-new-teeth-one-day).

## Why is there no single month on this page?

The 4-to-6-month interval belongs to 30 people in one mandibular series, and it was the interval before a metal or titanium framework. It was not the interval for the routine group. Healing, the opposing teeth, a repair, or a lost implant changed individual stories inside that same paper: some replacement implants were loaded when the final was placed, around 4 or 5 months in the cases they describe.<Cite id="malo-2003" />

A webpage that offered one month would be inventing a protocol the study did not use for everyone. The first weeks of eating and soreness are a different timeline, in [the first weeks after All-on-4](/blog/all-on-4-recovery-first-weeks). The visit sequence from consultation onward is on [what to expect](/what-to-expect).

## Why does Renew say a few months?

The [All-on-4 dental implants](/services/all-on-4-dental-implants) page says most patients receive implants and temporary teeth in one visit. It also says the span from the first consultation to the permanent bridge typically takes a few months, and that within a few months the implants integrate and the permanent bridge is placed. The phrase on that page about not waiting for months is the claim that people are not left without teeth. It sits next to the few-months line for the later bridge.<Cite id="renew-all-on-4" />

The implant surgeon places the implants. Tom Szarski, DD, is the denturist who designs and fits the teeth with the on-site lab. The free consultation, with a 3D scan, is where a personal sequence is discussed. This article can show the published range and the clinic's own wording. It cannot assign you a calendar date.
