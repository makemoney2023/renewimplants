## What does this clinic say is in the number?

The pricing page says every case is different: the teeth, the bone, and the restoration change the fee. It says you leave the free consultation with a complete price. It then lists what that one number covers: the free initial consultation, 3D scans and digital imaging, sedation when applicable, lab work designed and milled in the on-site lab, temporary teeth if needed, and follow-ups.<Cite id="renew-pricing" />

That list is the clinic speaking about its own quote. It is not a fee schedule, and it is not a study. If a line on a quote you received elsewhere is missing from this list, ask. Do not treat this article as the invoice.

The hub for cost questions is [pricing and financing](/pricing). How a visit is sequenced is on [what to expect](/what-to-expect). How dentures and implants differ, before you compare two quotes, is in [dentures versus dental implants](/blog/dentures-vs-dental-implants).

## Why the plan does not fill in the implant line?

The Dental Benefits Guide excludes implants and all implant-related procedures, including dentures supported by implants, and says those exclusions are not eligible for reconsideration.<Cite id="cdcp-guide" /> A quote for All-on-4 or for a denture on implants should not be written as if the plan pays that line. The longer explanation is [does the Canadian Dental Care Plan cover dental implants](/blog/does-the-canadian-dental-care-plan-cover-dental-implants).

The pricing page also says the office accepts the plan and major insurers and bills many claims directly.<Cite id="renew-pricing" /> Accepting the plan means the clinic can bill services the plan covers. It does not turn an excluded implant service into a covered one.

## What should you ask before you compare quotes?

Ask what is in the number, what is extra, whether sedation is in or out, whether the temporary teeth are in or out, and whether the final prosthesis is made in that clinic's lab or sent out. Those are the lines this clinic's page says it folds into one number.<Cite id="renew-pricing" /> Another clinic can write a quote differently. Compare the lines, not a headline.
