## What do published disease signs include?

Berglundh, Armitage, and the 2017 World Workshop define peri-implant health as the absence of erythema, bleeding on probing, swelling, and suppuration. Peri-implant mucositis is bleeding on gentle probing. Erythema, swelling, and suppuration may be there. They name plaque as the cause. Peri-implantitis is plaque-associated inflammation of the mucosa together with progressive loss of supporting bone. The clinical signs they list are bleeding on probing and/or suppuration, increased probing depths and/or recession, plus radiographic bone loss.<Cite id="berglundh-2018" />

That list is not a photograph you can take in a bathroom mirror. Probing depths and radiographs are clinic measurements.

## Is soreness after surgery the same list?

No. The workshop definitions are for peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis, not for the first days after an operation. This page does not turn a tight feeling, a bruise, or a tender gum into a failed implant. It also does not say a loose feeling proves failure. If the timing is the early weeks after All-on-4, read [what the studies do and do not time](/blog/all-on-4-recovery-first-weeks), and still call if you are worried. Longer follow-up, which is not the same question as this week's soreness, is in [how long All-on-4 implants last](/blog/how-long-do-all-on-4-implants-last).
