<Tldr>**No. The Canadian Dental Care Plan does not cover dental implants.** The Dental Benefits Guide excludes implants and all implant-related procedures, including dentures supported by implants, and says those exclusions are not eligible for reconsideration. Many removable dentures are listed separately, and some of them need preauthorization.</Tldr>

## Does the plan cover the implants themselves?

No. The Dental Benefits Guide excludes any crowns supported by implants and all implant-related procedures. It repeats that exclusion for complete and partial dentures supported by implants, and for implants and bone grafts as their own bullets. It says services on the exclusion list are not eligible for reconsideration.<Cite id="cdcp-guide" />

That is the line that matters for All-on-4, a fixed full-arch bridge, and a denture that clips onto implants. Those treatments depend on implants. The plan's exclusion is about the service, not about which clinic provides it.

## Which denture services are listed?

The coverage page lists removable prosthodontic care: complete dentures (standard and temporary), repairs, relines, rebases, tissue conditioning, complete immediate and overdentures with preauthorization, and partial dentures with preauthorization.<Cite id="cdcp-coverage" /> The same page says the plan covers a portion of the cost of many services, some services need preauthorization, and not all requests are approved.

The guide's frequency table also lists complete overdentures, with preauthorization, at one per arch in any 96 months, and immediate overdentures at one per arch in a lifetime.<Cite id="cdcp-guide" /> Put that next to the exclusion, not instead of it. An overdenture that sits on teeth or on the gums is not the same service as a denture supported by implants. The guide names implant-supported complete and partial dentures as exclusions.

## Why check the guide before you book?
