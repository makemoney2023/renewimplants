## What does this clinic say the lab does?

The [meet the team](/meet-your-dentist) page says Renew's sister clinic, Orléans Denture Clinic, operates a full on-site lab staffed by registered dental technicians. It names a PM7 milling machine, Asiga Professional Series digital printers, and 3Shape scanners, and it says the workflow is digital and that work is not outsourced.<Cite id="renew-team" />

The [All-on-4 dental implants](/services/all-on-4-dental-implants) page says the teeth are designed before treatment day with digital imaging and that on-site lab, including shape, shade, and fit. The same page says the implant surgeon places the implants and the denturist team attaches the teeth.<Cite id="renew-all-on-4" /> Who is allowed to do which part of that work is spelled out in [denturist and dentist roles for implant teeth](/blog/denturist-vs-dentist-for-implant-teeth).

## What do the studies say the lab is building?

Maló, Rangert, and Nobre describe the object, not the postal address of the laboratory. The same-day bridge was acrylic, reinforced with a metal strip, with at least 10 teeth. For the development group, the later prosthesis was cast metal in the first seven patients and a milled titanium Procera bridge in the other 23, made 4 to 6 months after surgery. The routine group mostly stayed in reinforced acrylic. Nine early acrylic bridges fractured and were repaired; after the acrylic was thickened, the routine group had no fractures in the reported follow-up.<Cite id="malo-2003" />

That is evidence about materials and timing. It is not evidence that a lab inside the clinic building produces higher implant survival than a lab down the street. [What All-on-4 meant in that protocol](/blog/what-is-all-on-4) keeps the surgical definition separate from the workshop.

## Why does Renew keep the lab in the same building?

The team page's reason is practical: the technicians, the mill, the printers, and the scanners are here, and the work is not sent out.<Cite id="renew-team" /> The denturist can design the teeth with the people who will mill and finish them, in the same plan as the surgeon who places the implants. A quote that itemizes lab work is discussed in [what is included in an implant quote](/blog/what-is-included-in-an-implant-quote).

Tom Szarski, DD, is the denturist. He does not place the implants. The free consultation includes a 3D scan. Keeping the lab in the building is a description of how this clinic is organized. It is not a survival statistic.
