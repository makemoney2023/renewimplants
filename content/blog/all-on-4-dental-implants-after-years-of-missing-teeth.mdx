**No — being without teeth for years does not automatically rule out All-on-4.** Your jawbone does shrink after tooth loss, but All-on-4 was designed around that: two of the four implants are angled to reach the denser bone that remains, which often avoids bone grafting. A 3D scan at your free consultation shows exactly how much bone you have.

## What happens to your jawbone over time?

**When a tooth is gone, the bone under it slowly shrinks because it no longer gets pressure from chewing.** Most of the change happens early, and it continues more slowly for years.

## Why doesn't long-term tooth loss stop treatment?

**Because All-on-4 places implants where the bone is strongest.** Even people who lost their teeth 10 or 20 years ago can often get a new set. The two front implants go in straight; the two back implants are tilted to avoid areas where bone has thinned, such as near the sinuses in the upper jaw or the nerve in the lower jaw.

## How does the All-on-4 method work?

**Four implants support a full arch of fixed teeth.** In many cases, a temporary bridge is attached the same day. The final bridge is made after the implants have healed.

## Who may be a good candidate?

**People who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both jaws, whose dentures move or affect daily life, or who want teeth that stay in.** Your health history, gum health, and bone volume all factor in, and only an in-person exam with a 3D scan can confirm candidacy.
