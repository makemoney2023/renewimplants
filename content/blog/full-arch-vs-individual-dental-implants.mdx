**Individual implants replace teeth one at a time; full arch implants replace an entire row of teeth on a few implants.** If you're missing one or a few teeth and the rest are healthy, individual implants usually make sense. If most teeth in a jaw are missing or failing, a full arch like All-on-4 restores the whole row with far fewer implants.

## What are individual dental implants?

**An individual implant replaces a single missing tooth.** A titanium post is placed in the jawbone, and after it heals, a custom crown is attached. Each missing tooth gets its own implant and crown.

## What are full arch dental implants?

**A full arch replaces most or all of the teeth in one jaw with a single bridge.** Several implants support the bridge, so you don't need one implant per tooth.

## How do they differ in practice?

**It comes down to how many teeth need replacing.** Replacing an entire row tooth by tooth would mean many implants and more surgery. A full arch spreads the bite across a few strategically placed implants, which is how All-on-4 restores a whole arch on four.
