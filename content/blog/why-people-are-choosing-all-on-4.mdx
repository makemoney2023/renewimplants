## Who is choosing All-on-4?

**The typical All-on-4 patient is over 50, and that matches what we see every day at Renew.** But more patients between 35 and 50 are choosing All-on-4 earlier, tired of the revolving door of temporary fixes. What they share isn't income. It's a tipping point: a denture that won't stay in, a bridge that failed again, or a smile they've stopped showing.

## Is it really not about the cost?

**For most people, the deciding factor is quality of life, not the price.** Ken M. put it simply after his procedure: the experience was life-changing — he could eat anything he wanted, had no more pain, and no longer worried about the look in people's eyes. Cost still matters, which is why we give one all-inclusive price at the free consultation and offer in-house payment plans.

## What do patients actually get from All-on-4?

**A fixed set of teeth that works like their own, from one treatment instead of many.** Patients tell us they want three things: to eat normally again, to stop hiding their smile, and to stop paying for repairs that don't last. A full arch on implants addresses all three because the teeth are anchored to the jaw rather than resting on the gums.

## Why All-on-4 instead of other implant options?

**All-on-4 restores an entire arch on four implants, two straight at the front and two angled at the back.** The angled implants reach denser bone, which often avoids bone grafting and shortens treatment. The approach was first published by Dr. Paulo Maló and colleagues in 2003, and it has long-term data behind it:
