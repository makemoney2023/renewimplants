**Yes — in many cases you can walk in with failing or missing teeth and walk out the same day with a fixed temporary smile.** The implants are placed, a temporary bridge is attached, and your final teeth are made once the implants have bonded with the bone. Whether it's right for you depends on your bone, gums, and overall health, which a 3D scan shows at your free consultation.

## How do same-day implants work?

**The implants and a temporary set of teeth are placed in one visit.** Traditionally, an implant is placed, the jaw heals around it for a few months, and then teeth are attached. With same-day implants, the temporary teeth go on right away, so you have a complete smile while the bone heals. The final bridge replaces the temporary once healing is complete.

## Who may qualify for same-day teeth?

**People with enough stable jawbone and healthy gums have the best chance.** The implants must be firm on the day of surgery to carry a temporary bridge. Your surgeon checks your jawbone, gums, and overall oral health with a 3D scan before recommending it.

## Why do people prefer same-day treatment?

**Leaving with teeth the same day saves time and removes the stress of going without.** Patients tell us it also helps them feel confident at work and with family during the healing months.

## What should you expect after the visit?

**Some soreness, swelling, or mild pain for a few days is normal.** Soft foods make the first weeks more comfortable while the implants bond with the bone. Your team schedules follow-ups to check healing and adjust the temporary teeth.

## When are one-day teeth not the right option?

**When the bone is too thin, gum disease is active, or other treatment is needed first.** In those cases a staged plan is safer, and it still ends with fixed teeth. The goal is the right result for the long term, not the fastest one.
