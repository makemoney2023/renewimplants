<Tldr>**Bone loss does not automatically rule out dental implants.** The jaw ridge shrinks after teeth are removed, especially in width during the first months. The All-on-4 concept was published as a way to use the bone that remains in an atrophic jaw. Whether that fits you is a scan question, not a slogan.</Tldr>

Human re-entry studies found the ridge changes quickly. At six months, mean buccal horizontal reduction was 3.79 mm and mean buccal vertical reduction was 1.24 mm. Mesial and distal vertical means were 0.84 mm and 0.80 mm.<Cite id="tan-2012" />

## How much bone changes after teeth are lost?

The bone that held a tooth loses the load of chewing. Tan, Wong, Wong, and Lang reviewed human studies that re-entered extraction sites. They reported horizontal bone loss of 29 to 63 percent and vertical bone loss of 11 to 22 percent after six months, with rapid reductions in the first three to six months. Horizontal percentage loss was about 32 percent at three months.<Cite id="tan-2012" />

Those are study means. They are not a measurement of your jaw. People who have been without teeth for years are the subject of [All-on-4 after years of missing teeth](/blog/all-on-4-dental-implants-after-years-of-missing-teeth). Diabetes and smoking, which are separate from bone volume, are in [dental implants with diabetes or smoking](/blog/dental-implants-with-diabetes-or-smoking).

## What was All-on-4 designed to do in a thin jaw?

Soto-Peñaloza and colleagues describe the concept as a way to maximize remnant bone in atrophic jaws, to provide immediate function, and to avoid regenerative procedures. The protocol they summarize places four implants in the anterior jaw, two axial and two angled posteriorly, to shorten the cantilever, supporting up to 12 teeth on a provisional fixed prosthesis that is loaded immediately.<Cite id="soto-2017" />

Their conclusion calls it a predictable approach for patients who do not prefer regenerative procedures. The same paper says the evidence is limited by sample size, follow-up, and patients lost along the way. This page does not repeat a survival percentage from that review, because the authors warn that the numbers can mislead.

The [All-on-4 service page](/services/all-on-4-dental-implants) is the clinic hub for that treatment. Candidacy for your mouth is discussed against [what to expect](/what-to-expect) at a consultation, after a 3D scan.

## Why a scan comes before a yes?

Tom Szarski, DD, is the denturist. He does not place the implants. The implant surgeon places them. The on-site lab builds the teeth. None of those roles can see bone density from a quiz answer. If someone told you the bone is gone, bring that history to the consultation and let the scan answer it.
