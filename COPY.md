The dental implant market has grown to roughly $8 billion globally, with projections showing continued acceleration through the end of the decade. All-on-4 procedures in particular have seen a surge in demand, with clinical studies reporting a 98.8% prosthetic survival rate — numbers that give patients and practitioners alike a lot of confidence.

But here's what the statistics don't capture: the real reason people are making this choice isn't financial. It's personal.

The typical All-on-4 patient is over 50 — and that tracks with what we see every day at Renew Implant Centre . Research shows that implant prevalence increases significantly after age 50, with the 65–74 age group representing one of the largest demographics for dental implant procedures. Nearly 69% of adults between 35 and 44 have already lost at least one permanent tooth, and by the time most people reach their 50s and 60s, the cumulative toll of decades of dental work — crowns, root canals, bridges, extractions — reaches a breaking point.

But it's not just seniors. More patients in the 35–50 range are opting for All-on-4 earlier, tired of the revolving door of temporary fixes. They're professionals, parents, and active people who don't want to spend another decade managing failing teeth.
