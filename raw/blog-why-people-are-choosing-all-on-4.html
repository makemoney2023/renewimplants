Why More People Are Choosing All-on-4 Dental Implants — And It's Not About the Price

More Canadians over 50 are choosing All-on-4 — not because of cost, but because of confidence, freedom, and quality of life.

Something is shifting in dentistry right now — and it has very little to do with price tags.

Across Canada and the United States, a growing number of adults are choosing All-on-4 dental implants over traditional dentures, bridges, and patchwork fixes. The dental implant market has grown to roughly $8 billion globally, with projections showing continued acceleration through the end of the decade. All-on-4 procedures in particular have seen a surge in demand, with clinical studies reporting a 98.8% prosthetic survival rate — numbers that give patients and practitioners alike a lot of confidence.

But here's what the statistics don't capture: the real reason people are making this choice isn't financial. It's personal.

Who's Getting All-on-4? The Average Patient Might Surprise You

The typical All-on-4 patient is over 50 — and that tracks with what we see every day at Renew Implant Centre. Research shows that implant prevalence increases significantly after age 50, with the 65–74 age group representing one of the largest demographics for dental implant procedures. Nearly 69% of adults between 35 and 44 have already lost at least one permanent tooth, and by the time most people reach their 50s and 60s, the cumulative toll of decades of dental work — crowns, root canals, bridges, extractions — reaches a breaking point.

But it's not just seniors. More patients in the 35–50 range are opting for All-on-4 earlier, tired of the revolving door of temporary fixes. They're professionals, parents, and active people who don't want to spend another decade managing failing teeth.

What they all share isn't a tax bracket. It's a tipping point.

It's Not About Cost — It's About Being Done

When we sit down with patients during their free consultation, the conversation almost never starts with money. It starts with exhaustion.

They're exhausted from:

  • Dentures that slip, click, or need adhesive every morning
  • Teeth that keep breaking no matter how much they spend on repairs
  • Avoiding foods they love — corn on the cob, steak, apples — because chewing is painful or embarrassing
  • Hiding their smile in photos, at family gatherings, in professional settings
  • The emotional weight of feeling judged every time they open their mouth

One of our patients, Ken M., put it simply after his All-on-4 procedure: the experience was life-changing — he could eat anything he wanted, no more pain, and no longer had to worry about the look in people's eyes when they noticed his teeth.

Another long-time patient, Bob C., had been coming to us for 14 years with a very difficult case. Through it all, we kept him informed and maintained his quality of life. Today, he has what he calls a perfect, healthy smile.

These stories aren't unusual. They're the norm. You can read more of them on our patient stories page.

The Real Value: Confidence, Freedom, and Function

When patients weigh the decision, the factors that tip the scale are almost always emotional and functional — not financial:

They want to eat normally again. All-on-4 implants restore up to 90% of natural bite force. That means real food — not soft-only diets dictated by loose dentures. For many patients, this alone changes their daily quality of life dramatically.

They want to stop hiding. We hear this constantly — people who haven't smiled in photos for years, who cover their mouth when they laugh, who've pulled back from social situations because of their teeth. Same-day dental implants mean they can walk in with broken or missing teeth and walk out the same day with a full, natural-looking smile.

They want one solution, not an endless cycle. Traditional approaches often mean bouncing between specialists — one office for extractions, another for grafting, another for the prosthetic. At Renew, it's one clinic, one team, one day. Tom Szarski, DD, and Dr. Alex handle everything under one roof, with a full onsite lab run by Registered Dental Technicians using 3D scanning and digital milling. No outsourcing. No runaround.

They want it to last. The titanium implants themselves can last a lifetime with proper care. The fixed bridge on top typically lasts 10–15 years. Compare that to dentures that need replacing every 5–8 years, with ongoing costs for adhesives, relines, and adjustments that add up quietly over time. When you break down a $20,000–$25,000 All-on-4 investment over 20 years, it works out to just a few dollars a day — less than a morning coffee.

Why All-on-4 Specifically?

There are plenty of implant options out there. What makes All-on-4 the one people are gravitating toward?

Fewer implants, same result. Traditional full-mouth implants might require 6–10 implants per arch. All-on-4 uses four strategically placed implants — two straight, two angled — to support an entire arch of fixed teeth. Fewer implants means less surgery, less healing time, and less cost.

Often no bone grafting required. The angled rear implants are specifically designed to work with the bone you already have, even if you've experienced some bone loss. This eliminates one of the biggest barriers — and biggest fears — that keep people stuck in dentures. Learn more about this on our full arch dental implants page.

Same-day results. In many cases, patients receive a fixed temporary prosthesis the same day as their procedure. No months spent in a temporary denture waiting for implants to heal. You leave the clinic with teeth that work.

Proven track record. Developed in the 1990s by Dr. Paulo Maló in collaboration with Nobel Biocare, All-on-4 has decades of clinical evidence behind it. Long-term studies consistently show success rates above 95% at the 10-year mark.

"I Should Have Done This Years Ago"

If there's one phrase we hear more than any other at Renew, it's this one. Stephen E., one of our patients, said exactly that after his implants were placed — he was so happy with the result and wished he hadn't waited so long.

The hesitation is understandable. It's a significant decision. But the regret almost always runs in one direction: not doing it sooner.

That's why we offer free consultations with no pressure and no obligation. We'll review your situation, explain your options in plain language, and give you clear, honest pricing so you can decide at your own pace.

If you're scared of the dentist, we get it — and you won't be judged for it. We offer sedation dentistry so you can sleep comfortably through the entire procedure. Many patients tell us it was the easiest dental experience of their life.
