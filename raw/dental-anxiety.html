/ WE GET IT

Most of Our Patients Felt Exactly the Way You Do Right Now

Maybe it was a bad experience years ago. Maybe it was a dentist who made you feel embarrassed about your teeth. Maybe the thought of sitting in that chair just makes your stomach drop.

Whatever brought you here — you're not alone. Dental anxiety is one of the most common reasons people put off treatment, sometimes for years or even decades. And by the time they walk through our door, most of our patients are carrying a mix of fear, frustration, and guilt about waiting so long.

Here's what we want you to know: we don't care how long it's been. We don't care how your teeth got this way. We care about helping you move forward — at your pace, on your terms, without a single lecture.

/ THE RENEW APPROACH

We Built This Clinic for People Like You

No Judgment. No Lectures. Ever.

You'll never hear "why did you wait so long?" from anyone on our team. We understand that life gets in the way, fear is real, and the last thing you need is guilt. You'll be treated with kindness and respect from the moment you walk in.

You Set the Pace

Your first visit is just a conversation. No one touches your mouth unless you say so. We explain everything before we do it, answer every question, and let you decide when — and if — you're ready to move forward.

Sedation Is Always an Option

If the thought of being awake during treatment makes you anxious, you don't have to be. We offer sedation dentistry so you can sleep through the entire procedure and wake up with the hard part already done.

/ SLEEP THROUGH IT

You Don't Have to Be Awake for Any of It

Many of our most anxious patients tell us that sedation was the thing that finally made treatment possible. And once it's done, most of them say the same thing: "I wish I'd done this years ago."

At Renew, sedation is administered by our experienced team in a safe, monitored environment. You'll drift off before the procedure begins and wake up when it's over — often with your new smile already in place.

It's not about being brave. It's about finding the path that works for you.

/ WHAT TO EXPECT

Your First Visit — No Surprises, No Pressure

1

We Just Talk

Your consultation starts with a conversation — not an exam. We sit down, listen to what you've been through, and answer your questions in plain English. If you want to look around the clinic first, that's fine too. There's coffee in the waiting room.

2

A Gentle Assessment

When you're comfortable, we take a quick look using 3D scanning — no painful impressions, no discomfort. We'll explain what we see, what your options are, and what everything costs. No surprises.

3

You Decide

There's no pressure to book anything. You take the information home, think it over, talk to your family. When you're ready — if you're ready — we're here.
