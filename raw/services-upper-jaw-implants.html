/ THE CHALLENGE

Upper Dentures Are the Ones People Hate Most

If you wear an upper denture, you already know. The plate that covers your palate blocks your sense of taste. The adhesive never holds the way it should. You're constantly aware it's there — every meal, every conversation, every laugh is filtered through the fear that something will shift.

And the longer you wait, the more bone you lose. Your face starts to change shape. Your denture fits worse and worse. It's a cycle that doesn't fix itself.

Upper jaw implants break that cycle. They replace the palate plate with fixed teeth anchored directly to your jawbone — teeth that look, feel, and function like the ones you were born with.

/ WHAT MAKES UPPER UNIQUE

Upper Jaw Implants Require Specific Expertise

Less Bone to Work With

The upper jaw is naturally less dense than the lower, and it sits beneath the sinus cavity. That means implant placement requires precision planning and an experienced surgical team — which is exactly what you get at Renew.

Sinus Considerations

In some cases, the sinus cavity is close to where implants need to go. Our team uses 3D scanning to map the exact anatomy of your upper jaw, so we can place implants safely and effectively — often without the need for sinus lifts or bone grafts.

The Palate Factor

One of the biggest benefits of upper jaw implants is eliminating the palate plate entirely. That means your sense of taste comes back, food feels normal again, and you can speak without that thick, awkward feeling on the roof of your mouth.

/ UPPER JAW SOLUTIONS

We'll Help You Choose the Right Approach

Not every upper jaw case is the same. Some patients are candidates for All-on-4 — four implants supporting a full fixed bridge. Others may benefit from six or more implants depending on bone density and anatomy. And for patients who prefer a removable option, snap-on overdentures can provide a dramatic improvement over a traditional denture.

During your free consultation, Tom and Dr. Alex will evaluate your specific situation using 3D scans and recommend the approach that gives you the best long-term result — not the most expensive one.

  • All-on-4 upper arch — fixed, permanent teeth on four implants
  • Full arch on 6+ implants — for cases needing additional support
  • Snap-on overdentures — implant-retained, removable for cleaning
  • No-palate design — every option eliminates the palate plate
  • Same day options available — teeth placed in one visit
