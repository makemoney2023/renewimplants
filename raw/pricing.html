/ HOW WE DO PRICING

Why We Don't List Prices on This Page

We know you came here looking for a number — and we respect that. The truth is, every patient's case is different. The condition of your teeth, the amount of bone you have, the type of restoration you need — all of these affect the final cost.

What we can promise you is this: when you come in for your free consultation, you'll leave with a complete, all-inclusive price. No guessing. No "we'll let you know later." No surprise bills after the fact.

We give you one number that covers everything — the implants, the lab work, the follow-ups. And because our onsite lab handles everything in-house, we're often able to offer pricing that's more competitive than clinics that outsource their work.

/ INSURANCE & COVERAGE

We Work With Your Coverage — So You Don't Have To

CDCP Accepted

We accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP). If you're eligible, our office team will help you navigate the process and make sure your coverage is applied to your treatment.

All Major Insurance

We work with all major dental insurance providers in Canada. Our staff handles the paperwork and submits claims directly on your behalf — so you can focus on your smile, not forms.

Direct Billing

No paying out of pocket and waiting for reimbursement. We bill your insurance company directly wherever possible, so your out-of-pocket costs are clear from day one.

/ PAYMENT PLANS

Financing That Works for You — Not Against You

We believe that cost shouldn't be the thing standing between you and a smile you can trust. That's why we offer in-house payment plans designed to make treatment accessible — without the stress of third-party lenders or credit checks.

During your consultation, our team will walk you through all your financing options, help you understand what your insurance covers, and put together a payment plan that fits your budget.

The bottom line: if you're ready for treatment, we'll find a way to make it work.
