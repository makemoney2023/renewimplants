Sedation dentistry lets you relax — or sleep — through your entire procedure. No anxiety, no pain, no memory of the visit. Just results.

Maybe it's been years since you've sat in a dental chair. Maybe a past experience left you with a knot in your stomach every time you think about making an appointment. Maybe you know your teeth need help, but the fear of the procedure keeps you stuck.

You're not alone. Dental anxiety affects millions of adults, and it's one of the most common reasons people delay the care they need. At Renew, we hear it every week — and we never judge. Not for how long it's been, not for how your teeth got this way, and not for the fear you carry.

Sedation dentistry exists for exactly this reason. It removes the barrier between you and the care you deserve — so the only thing you have to do is show up. We'll handle the rest.

- - Oral Sedation You take a prescribed medication before your appointment that helps you feel calm, drowsy, and deeply relaxed. You'll be awake but won't feel anxious — and most patients remember very little of the procedure afterward. It's the most common option for patients who want to take the edge off.

- IV Sedation Administered by a trained sedation professional, IV sedation puts you into a deep state of relaxation. You'll drift in and out of sleep throughout the procedure and likely won't remember any of it. It's ideal for longer treatments like All-on-4 or full arch implants.

General Anaesthesia For patients who want to be completely asleep, general anaesthesia is available for more complex cases. You're fully unconscious throughout the procedure and wake up when everything is done. It's the deepest level of sedation and is performed with a qualified anaesthesia team.

We'll give you clear instructions ahead of time — what to eat (or not eat), when to take your medication if you're doing oral sedation, and what to wear. You'll need someone to drive you home, so plan for a ride.

When you walk in, our team is ready for you. We'll get you settled, answer any last-minute questions, and administer the sedation. Within minutes, you'll feel calm, relaxed, and ready.

This is the part you won't remember. Whether it takes one hour or four, you'll be comfortable the entire time. Dr. Alex and Tom handle the implants and teeth while you rest.

When it's over, we'll gently bring you back. You'll be groggy but comfortable. Your ride takes you home, and within a day or two you're back to normal — with a brand new smile and no memory of the procedure.

All sedation at Renew is administered by trained professionals and monitored throughout the procedure. We'll review your medical history during your consultation and recommend the level of sedation that's safest and most appropriate for you.

It depends on the type of sedation. Oral sedation keeps you deeply relaxed but technically awake. IV sedation puts most patients in and out of sleep. General anaesthesia means you're fully asleep.
