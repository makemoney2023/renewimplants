# What's the Difference Between Full-Arch Dental Implants and Individual Implants?

## What Are Individual Dental Implants?
Individual dental implants replace one missing tooth at a time. A titanium post is placed in the jawbone; after healing, a custom crown is attached.

## What Are Full-Arch Dental Implants?
Full-arch dental implants replace most or all teeth in an upper or lower arch. Several implants support a full set of replacement teeth.

## How Do They Differ?
Individual implants replace single teeth; full-arch implants replace an entire row using fewer implant posts.
