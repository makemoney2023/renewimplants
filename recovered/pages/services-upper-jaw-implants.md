No adhesive. No slipping. Upper jaw implants give you fixed, natural-looking teeth you never have to take out.

If you wear an upper denture , you already know. The plate that covers your palate blocks your sense of taste. The adhesive never holds the way it should. You're constantly aware it's there — every meal, every conversation, every laugh is filtered through the fear that something will shift.

And the longer you wait, the more bone you lose. Your face starts to change shape. Your denture fits worse and worse. It's a cycle that doesn't fix itself.

Upper jaw implants break that cycle. They replace the palate plate with fixed teeth anchored directly to your jawbone — teeth that look, feel, and function like the ones you were born with.

Less Bone to Work With The upper jaw is naturally less dense than the lower, and it sits beneath the sinus cavity. That means implant placement requires precision planning and an experienced surgical team — which is exactly what you get at Renew.

Sinus Considerations In some cases, the sinus cavity is close to where implants need to go. Our team uses 3D scanning to map the exact anatomy of your upper jaw, so we can place implants safely and effectively — often without the need for sinus lifts or bone grafts.

- - The Palate Factor One of the biggest benefits of upper jaw implants is eliminating the palate plate entirely. That means your sense of taste comes back, food feels normal again, and you can speak without that thick, awkward feeling on the roof of your mouth.

Not every upper jaw case is the same. Some patients are candidates for All-on-4 — four implants supporting a full fixed bridge. Others may benefit from six or more implants depending on bone density and anatomy. And for patients who prefer a removable option, snap-on overdentures can provide a dramatic improvement over a traditional denture. During your free consultation , Tom and Dr. Alex will evaluate your specific situation using 3D scans and recommend the approach that gives you the best long-term result — not the most expensive one.

Upper jaw implants involve careful coordination between the surgeon, the denturist, and the lab. At most clinics, that means three different offices, three different timelines, and a lot of hoping everyone's on the same page.

At Renew, all three work together under one roof. Dr. Alex places the implants. Tom designs and fits your teeth. Our onsite lab fabricates everything right here. That coordination is what produces reliable, same day results — especially in complex upper jaw cases.
