# Can You Really Walk Out with New Teeth in One Day?

Learn how same-day dental implants work, who may qualify, what to expect during recovery, and whether one-day teeth are right for you.

## How Do Same-Day Implants Work?
Getting dental implants usually takes a few months. First, the dentist places a small titanium implant into the jawbone. Over time, the jawbone grows around the implant and holds it firmly in place. After the area has healed, the final tooth or teeth are attached.

With same-day implants, part of the treatment can be completed in one visit. The dentist may place the implants and give you a temporary set of teeth on the same day. This means you can leave the clinic with a complete smile while your mouth heals.

The temporary teeth are not your final teeth. They help you smile, speak, and eat more comfortably during the healing period. Once your mouth has fully healed, your dentist will replace them with your permanent teeth.

## Who May Qualify?
Not everyone is a good fit for same-day implants. A dentist will first check your jawbone, gums, and the overall health of your mouth. People with healthy gums and enough jawbone support may have a better chance of getting this treatment.

## Why Do People Like Same-Day Treatment?
Many people like the idea of leaving with teeth right away because it can save time and reduce stress. It can also help a person feel more confident in social settings.

## What To Expect After The Visit?
After getting dental implants, your mouth may feel sore for a few days. You may also notice some swelling or mild pain. Eating soft foods can make you feel more comfortable while your mouth starts to heal.

## When Are One-Day Teeth Not the Right Option?
Same-day implants are not suitable for everyone. If your jawbone is not strong enough, you have gum disease, or your mouth needs more treatment before implants, your dentist may recommend a longer treatment plan.
