# Can You Get All-on-4 Dental Implants If You've Been Missing Teeth for Years?

Missing Teeth for Years Does Not Always Mean You Cannot Get All-on-4 Dental Implants.

## What Happens to Your Jawbone Over Time?
When a tooth is gone, the bone under it slowly shrinks because it no longer gets pressure from chewing. Over several years, that bone loss can add up.

## Why Long-Term Tooth Loss Doesn't Stop Treatment?
Even people who lost their teeth 10 or 20 years ago can often get a new set. The All-on-4 method uses four implants placed at special angles to reach stronger areas of the jaw.

## How the All-on-4 Method Works?
The All-on-4 treatment restores a full arch of teeth using just four strategically placed dental implants. In many cases, temporary teeth can be attached the same day.

## Who May Be a Good Candidate?
- Have lost most or all teeth in one or both arches
- Have dentures that move, feel uncomfortable, or affect daily activities
- Want permanent teeth that stay in place instead of removable dentures
