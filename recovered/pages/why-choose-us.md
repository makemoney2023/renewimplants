Most patients who walk through our doors have already been to other clinics. They've been bounced between specialists, waited months for answers, and spent thousands on work that didn't last. Renew was built to be different — and you'll feel it the moment you walk in.

Everything Under One Roof Your consultation, your surgery, your lab work, your follow-ups — it all happens here. No driving across town. No repeating your story to a new face. One clinic, one team, start to finish.

Full Onsite Dental Lab Our lab is right here in the building — equipped with 3D scanners, precision milling machines, and digital printers. Your implants are designed, crafted, and fitted by the same team that planned your treatment. Nothing is outsourced.

20+ Years of Experience Tom Szarski has been restoring smiles in Ottawa for over two decades, and his family has been in dentistry for four generations. You're not a test case — you're in experienced hands.

No Judgment. Ever. We don't care how long it's been. We don't care how your teeth got this way. We care about where you want to go — and we'll help you get there with dignity and respect.

Most implant clinics send your case to an outside lab. That means delays, miscommunication, and extra appointments when something doesn't fit quite right.

At Renew, our onsite lab is run by Registered Dental Technicians using some of the best equipment in the industry — including the PM7 milling machine, Asiga digital printers, and 3Shape scanners. Every restoration is designed, milled, and finished in-house, which means faster turnaround, better quality control, and a fit that's right the first time.

It's one of the biggest reasons patients tell us the experience at Renew felt different from day one.

Straight Answers in Plain English No jargon. No confusing treatment codes. We explain exactly what's going on, what your options are, and what everything costs — before anything begins. You'll never be surprised by a bill.

Your Pace, Not Ours We don't pressure anyone into treatment. You'll get a clear plan and an honest recommendation — and then you decide when you're ready. No sales pitch. No countdown timers.

A Team That Feels Like Family Our patients don't feel like patients — they feel like they belong. From the front desk to the treatment room, everyone at Renew treats you the way they'd want their own family treated.
