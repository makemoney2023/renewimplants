You've been dealing with broken, loose, or missing teeth for months — maybe years. Every meal is a calculation. Every conversation, a moment of self-consciousness. You've been told you need implants, but the idea of months of procedures, temporary dentures, and multiple offices kept you from moving forward.

Same day dental implants change all of that.

You arrive in the morning with teeth that don't work. By the afternoon, you leave with a full set of fixed, natural-looking teeth — placed by Dr. Alex and designed in our onsite lab by Tom and his team. One visit. One clinic. One team that handles everything.

This isn't a shortcut. It's modern implant dentistry done right — with the technology and expertise to give you a predictable, lasting result in a fraction of the time.

Before your procedure, we use 3D digital scans to map your jawbone and plan the exact position of every implant. There's no guesswork — your surgery and your new teeth are planned together, in advance, using the same data.

An Onsite Lab That Moves With You Most clinics send your case to an outside lab and wait days or weeks for your teeth. At Renew, our lab is right here — equipped with PM7 milling machines, Asiga printers, and 3Shape scanners. Your teeth are designed and fabricated in the same building where they're placed.

A Surgeon and Denturist, Side by Side Dr. Alex places the implants. Tom designs and fits the teeth. They work together on every case — no miscommunication, no delays, no referrals. That collaboration is what makes same day results possible without compromising quality.

Not sure if you're a candidate? That's exactly what the free consultation is for. We'll take a look, talk through your options, and give you a straight answer — no pressure, no sales pitch.

You walk in knowing today is the day. Your treatment plan is already set. If you've chosen sedation , you'll be comfortably asleep within minutes. If not, local anaesthesia keeps you completely numb and at ease.

Dr. Alex places the implants into your jawbone using the 3D surgical plan. The procedure is precise and efficient — most placements are completed within a couple of hours.

While you're still here, Tom and his team in the onsite lab design and attach your new set of teeth to the implants. You'll see your new smile in the mirror before you leave.

You leave the clinic with a full set of fixed, functional teeth. We send you home with clear aftercare instructions and schedule your follow-up . Most patients are back to eating soft foods within a day or two.

Here's the truth: same day implants aren't possible at most clinics. Why? Because they rely on outside labs, external surgeons, and separate appointments that stretch across weeks.

At Renew, the surgeon, the denturist, and the lab all work together under one roof. That's not a marketing line — it's the reason we can deliver predictable same day results while other clinics simply can't.

When the team that plans your case, places your implants, and builds your teeth is all in the same room, things move faster, communication is better, and the result is something everyone stands behind.
