Full arch dental implants are for patients who need an entire set of upper or lower teeth replaced — or both. Instead of relying on removable dentures that slip, click, and limit what you can eat, full arch implants give you a permanent set of fixed teeth anchored directly to your jawbone.

Think of it as going from a temporary fix to a permanent solution. You brush them like normal teeth. You eat what you want. You smile without thinking about it. And you never take them out.

Whether you're replacing teeth that have been failing for years, upgrading from dentures you can't stand, or dealing with the aftermath of another clinic's work — full arch restoration gives you a clean slate and a smile that lasts.

Full arch restoration isn't a one-step procedure — it involves surgery, prosthetic design, lab fabrication, and multiple fitting appointments. At most clinics, that means coordinating between a surgeon, a dentist, and an outside lab — all on different schedules, in different buildings, with different priorities.

Our lab fabricates your teeth on site with a PM7 milling machine, Asiga printers, and 3Shape scanners.
