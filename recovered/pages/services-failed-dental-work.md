You trusted a professional. You followed the instructions. You showed up for every appointment. And somehow, you're right back where you started — or worse.

Maybe the implant didn't integrate with the bone. Maybe the bridge cracked after a few years. Maybe the crowns keep loosening and nobody can explain why. Or maybe you went to a clinic that promised the world and delivered something that barely lasted.

Whatever happened, you're not to blame. And you're not stuck. Failed dental work can almost always be corrected — it just takes the right team, the right plan, and the honesty to tell you what actually went wrong.

That's what we do at Renew.

Implants that didn't fuse to the bone, were placed incorrectly, or developed infection. These can often be removed, the site can heal, and new implants can be placed properly.

- Broken Bridges & Crowns Poorly designed prosthetics that crack, chip, or fall off. We see bridges built on compromised teeth, crowns that were never a good fit, and lab work that wasn't up to standard.

- - Ill-Fitting Dentures Dentures made without proper measurements or that were never adjusted after placement. If your denture rocks, slips, or causes sore spots that won't heal — it wasn't made right.

Incomplete Treatment Treatment plans that were started but never finished — temporary teeth that became permanent by default, implants placed without a prosthetic on top, or a plan that fell apart when the clinic closed or the provider left.

We don't sugarcoat it. Tom and Dr. Alex will take 3D scans, review your history, and tell you exactly what went wrong and what it'll take to fix it. If we can save existing work, we will. If we can't, we'll explain why and what comes next.

We build a custom treatment plan based on where you are now — not where you should have been. That includes a realistic timeline, an all-inclusive price, and the sedation options to make it comfortable if you're anxious about going through another procedure.

Whether it's removing a failed implant and starting fresh, replacing a broken bridge with a full arch restoration, or completely redoing someone else's work — we handle it all under one roof. Same surgeon, same denturist, same lab. No bouncing between offices.

One of the most common reasons dental work fails is poor coordination. The surgeon places the implants but doesn't communicate well with the lab. The lab builds a prosthetic that doesn't fit the surgical plan. And the patient pays the price.

At Renew, your surgeon, your denturist, and your lab all work together in the same building — on the same page, every step of the way. Dr. Alex places the implants. Tom designs and fits the teeth. Our onsite lab fabricates everything right here. That coordination is the difference between work that holds up and work that falls apart.
