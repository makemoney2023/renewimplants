A Full Set of Teeth. One Day. All-on-4 dental implants give you a complete, fixed smile — designed, crafted, and placed right here in Orléans. No denture adhesive. No months of waiting. Just teeth you can trust.

All-on-4 is exactly what it sounds like — a full set of upper or lower teeth, permanently fixed to just four dental implants placed directly into your jawbone.

Unlike removable dentures , these teeth stay in. You brush them like natural teeth. You eat whatever you want — steak, corn on the cob, apples — without a second thought. No clicking, no slipping, no adhesive.

The four implants are placed at precise angles to maximize your existing bone, which means most patients don't need bone grafting at all. That's fewer procedures, less healing time, and a faster path to the smile you've been putting off.

Your Teeth Are Failing You've had crown after crown, root canal after root canal — and you're still dealing with pain, infection, or teeth that keep breaking. All-on-4 replaces everything at once, so you can stop chasing problems and start fresh.

The adhesive, the slipping, the diet restrictions — you've had enough. All-on-4 gives you fixed teeth that feel and function like your own. No more taking your teeth out at night.

You've Been Told You Need Bone Grafting Traditional implants often require months of grafting and healing. All-on-4 is designed to work with the bone you already have — which means many patients who were told "no" somewhere else are actually great candidates here.

You sit down with Tom and the team for a free, no-pressure consultation. We listen to what you've been through, take 3D scans of your jaw, and talk through your options in plain English. You'll leave with a clear plan and an honest price — no surprises.

Using digital imaging and our onsite lab, we design your new teeth before treatment day. You'll see exactly what your smile will look like and have a say in every detail — shape, shade, and fit.

Dr. Alex places four implants into your jaw, and Tom's team attaches your new set of teeth — all in the same visit. You walk in with failing teeth and walk out with a smile you can trust. That's it.

We follow up with you to make sure everything feels right. Within a few months, your implants fully integrate with your bone and your permanent bridge is placed. From there, it's just regular checkups — like having natural teeth again.

Most All-on-4 patients spend months bouncing between a dentist, a surgeon, and an outside lab — repeating their story at every stop. It's exhausting, and it leads to miscommunication and delays.

At Renew, your consultation, your surgery, your lab work, and your follow-ups all happen in one building with one team. Our onsite lab is equipped with a PM7 milling machine, Asiga digital printers, and 3Shape scanners — so your teeth are designed and fabricated right here, by the people who know your case.

That means faster turnaround, better quality control, and a team that's with you from start to finish.

These are real patients who walked in with broken, missing, or failing teeth — and walked out with a brand-new smile. No stock photos. No exaggerations. Just results.
